# Carouseln — AI Social Media Carousel Studio

**Carouseln** is a premium, AI-powered content creation studio designed for solo creators, marketing agencies, and startups. It empowers you to generate stunning, high-converting social media carousels for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook in seconds.

Visit the live platform at: **[carouseln.com](https://carouseln.com)**

---

## ⚡ The Concept

Social media platforms thrive on visual storytelling, but creating consistent, high-impact slide decks is slow and tedious. 

Carouseln solves this by letting you define your **Brand DNA** (logos, typography guidelines, and color palettes) once. Our AI engine then writes, structures, and styles multi-slide carousels dynamically based on your brand rules.

---

## 🎨 Core Features

* **Brand DNA Engine**: Save your primary colors, background preferences, typography pairings, website handle, and brand logo. The generator styles every slide automatically to match.
* **AI Content Generation**: Powered by advanced Gemini models. Input a single topic, and the engine generates a structured 5-to-7-slide sequence complete with hooks, lessons, quote sections, stats, and call-to-actions.
* **Neobrutalist Aesthetics**: Bold borders, flat offset shadows, and premium typography pairings (such as *Space Grotesk*, *Outfit*, *Syne*, and *Cormorant Garamond*).
* **Multi-Format Export**: Export your completed carousels instantly as high-resolution PNG images or print-ready PDFs, pre-optimized for social platforms.
* **Privacy by Design**: Drafts and configurations are securely stored, with robust compliance and data control configurations.
